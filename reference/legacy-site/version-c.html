Our process

What working with us looks like

  1. Discovery

    A consultant maps your current process, pain points and goals, and agrees clear objectives with you before anything is built.

  2. Build

    Developers configure and code in a sandbox to Salesforce standards, with regular reviews so there are no surprises.

  3. Go-live & training

    We test with your users, go live, and run role-based training so people actually use the new setup.

  4. Ongoing support

    Raise a ticket any time, 24×7. We fix issues and keep tuning the org as your business changes.
