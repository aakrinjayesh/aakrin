# Aakrin — Positioning

## Primary positioning

Aakrin is a **Salesforce engineering company that helps Salesforce consulting partners extend their capabilities** across Salesforce's major clouds and platforms.

Primary expertise:

- Sales Cloud
- Service Cloud
- Experience Cloud
- Marketing Cloud
- Field Service
- Data Cloud
- Agentforce

## Secondary positioning

Aakrin helps businesses:

- Implement Salesforce
- Integrate Salesforce
- Customize Salesforce
- Develop Salesforce solutions
- Migrate and manage Salesforce data
- Optimize Salesforce
- Build and maintain Salesforce solutions
- Apply Agentforce and AI capabilities

## Strategic distinction

The website must NOT present Aakrin as merely another generic Salesforce consulting company.

The central differentiation should communicate engineering depth and the ability to act as an extension of Salesforce consulting teams.

The messaging should support two audiences:

### Audience A — Salesforce consulting partners

Core value:

> Extend your Salesforce delivery capabilities without having to build every specialist capability in-house.

Potential needs:

- Additional engineering capacity
- Complex customization
- Integration
- Development
- Data
- Agentforce
- Technical delivery support
- Specialist execution

### Audience B — Businesses

Core value:

> Implement, connect, customize, optimize, and operate Salesforce around the way the business actually works.

Do not force both audiences into one confusing hero message. The homepage should establish the engineering-company positioning first and make the two audience paths obvious.

## Positioning hierarchy

Primary:
Salesforce Engineering

Secondary:
Salesforce Consulting + Implementation + Development + Integration + AI/Agentforce

Proof:
Projects, clients, certifications, case studies, measurable outcomes

## Message rule

Do not use vague statements as the only headline, such as:

> Get Salesforce working the way your business does.

That can remain as supporting copy/tagline material, but the primary hero must communicate Salesforce and Aakrin's engineering capability immediately.
