# Aakrin — Homepage Specification

## Goal

The homepage must answer five questions quickly:

1. What is Aakrin?
2. What does Aakrin do?
3. Who does Aakrin help?
4. Why should I trust Aakrin?
5. What should I do next?

## Hero

The hero must communicate:

- Salesforce
- engineering capability
- primary audience/value
- secondary business capability
- CTA

Recommended conceptual direction:

> Salesforce engineering that extends what your team can deliver.

Supporting message should explain that Aakrin helps Salesforce consulting partners extend capabilities across Sales, Service, Marketing, Data Cloud, Agentforce and more, while also helping businesses implement, integrate, customize and optimize Salesforce.

Do not copy this wording blindly. Treat it as the messaging direction.

### Hero CTAs

Primary:
Book a Salesforce Consultation

Secondary:
Tell us what you need

### Hero proof

Use validated metrics only.

Currently known candidates:
- 200+ projects
- 80+ active clients
- 14+ certifications
- 98% satisfaction

These must be validated before final publication.

## Audience section

Create two clear paths:

### For Salesforce Consulting Partners
Extend your delivery capabilities with Aakrin's engineering expertise.

### For Businesses
Implement, integrate, customize and optimize Salesforce around your business.

Each path should have a relevant CTA.

## Trust section

Client logos may be used because the user confirmed permission to display them.

Current known logos include:
- Infosys
- Abbott
- Yash Technologies
- The Hackett Group
- MOURI Tech
- eMudhra
- AstroPay
- Artech
- Zessta
- Nvish

Do not add new logos without confirmation.

## Services section

Must explicitly show services.

Do not rely only on problem-based cards.

## Salesforce expertise section

Show:
- Sales Cloud
- Service Cloud
- Experience Cloud
- Marketing Cloud
- Field Service
- Data Cloud
- Agentforce

## Why Aakrin

Focus on engineering differentiation:

- Salesforce specialization
- Engineering capability
- Partner extension model
- Cross-cloud expertise
- AI / Agentforce capability
- End-to-end delivery

Avoid unsupported claims such as "best", "largest", or "world-class".

## Industries

Current confirmed priority industries:
- Manufacturing
- Hi-Tech
- Real Estate

Other industries can be added only when confirmed.

Do not imply deep specialization in every industry unless proof exists.

## Results

Use measurable outcomes wherever possible.

Current case-study details need additional user-provided information.

Known existing theme:
- Rebuilding customer support on Service Cloud
- Outcome currently described only as a significant rise in customer satisfaction

Do not invent the missing metrics.

## Process

Keep process concise:

Discover
→ Plan
→ Build
→ Launch
→ Optimize

Adapt wording if the actual sales/delivery process requires it.

## Quiz

The quiz is a lead qualification mechanism, not a generic personality quiz.

See `08-QUIZ.md`.

## Final CTA

The visitor should have a simple choice:

Book a Salesforce Consultation
or
Tell us what you need

The page should not finish with a confusing collection of unrelated CTAs.
