# Aakrin — Information Architecture

## Recommended top navigation

Primary:

- Services
- Salesforce
- Industries
- Results
- Insights
- About

Primary CTA:
- Book a Salesforce Consultation

Secondary CTA:
- Tell us what you need

Avoid a navigation structure where Services is hidden inside a generic "How we help" label.

## Homepage hierarchy

Recommended order:

1. Header
2. Hero / positioning
3. Audience paths
4. Trust / client proof
5. Services
6. Salesforce expertise
7. Why Aakrin / engineering advantage
8. Industries
9. Results / case studies
10. How we work
11. Qualification quiz
12. Final CTA
13. Footer

The exact ordering can be adjusted after implementation testing, but the conceptual hierarchy should remain.

## Content hierarchy

The website should clearly distinguish:

- What Aakrin is
- Who Aakrin helps
- What Aakrin does
- Salesforce platforms Aakrin works with
- Industries Aakrin serves
- Proof of capability
- How engagement works
- What the visitor should do next

## Blog

Blog/Insights should support SEO and authority but should not dominate the homepage above core conversion and credibility sections.
