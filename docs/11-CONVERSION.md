## Avoid CTA fragmentation

Do not use a unique CTA for every card such as:

- Fix my setup
- Scope my feature
- Plan my move
- Connect my systems
- Get support

Those labels can be useful in tightly contextual sections, but the overall site should maintain recognizable CTA hierarchy.

## Lead quality

The quiz should improve qualification rather than merely increase form submissions.

Lead context should include:
- visitor type / situation
- requested capability
- source/UTM where available

## Existing enquiry form

The existing enquiry form is planned for future update.

When implemented, it should become a real website submission flow rather than relying primarily on `mailto:`.

Recommended future architecture:

Website form
→ backend/API
→ lead storage/CRM
→ internal notification
→ visitor acknowledgement

Do not block the homepage redesign on the final backend implementation.
