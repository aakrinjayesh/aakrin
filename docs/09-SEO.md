# Aakrin — SEO Specification

## Primary SEO direction

Aakrin is targeting global search demand around Salesforce consulting, implementation, development, integration, migration, Agentforce, and related services.

Priority keyword themes include:

- Salesforce consulting company
- Salesforce consultant
- Salesforce implementation partner
- Salesforce development company
- Salesforce integration services
- Salesforce Agentforce consulting
- Salesforce consulting India
- Salesforce migration services
- Salesforce engineering
- Salesforce AI / Agentforce
- Salesforce Data Cloud

Exact keyword targeting should be assigned per page rather than stuffing all terms onto the homepage.

## Homepage SEO

The title should clearly communicate:

Aakrin + Salesforce + engineering/consulting positioning.

The meta description should communicate:
- Salesforce expertise
- engineering/services
- target business value
- global positioning

Do not keyword-stuff.

## Page architecture

Create dedicated SEO pages for important service/platform/industry terms.

Examples:

/services/salesforce-consulting
/services/salesforce-implementation
/services/salesforce-development
/services/salesforce-integration
/services/data-migration
/services/agentforce

/salesforce/sales-cloud
/salesforce/service-cloud
/salesforce/experience-cloud
/salesforce/marketing-cloud
/salesforce/field-service
/salesforce/data-cloud
/salesforce/agentforce

/industries/manufacturing
/industries/hi-tech
/industries/real-estate

Only publish pages when enough unique, useful content exists.

## Technical SEO

Implement:

- semantic HTML
- one clear H1 per page
- logical H2/H3 hierarchy
- title/meta per page
- canonical URLs
- Open Graph metadata
- sitemap
- robots.txt
- structured data where appropriate
- descriptive internal links
- optimized images
- fast loading
- accessible markup

## Structured data

Evaluate appropriate schema such as:

- Organization
- ProfessionalService
- Service
- BreadcrumbList
- Article for blog content

Do not add fake review/rating schema.

## Internal linking

Homepage should link naturally to:

- services
- Salesforce expertise/platform pages
- industries
- case studies
- insights
- contact

Service pages should link to related Salesforce platforms and case studies.

## Content quality

Every SEO page must provide genuinely useful information.

Do not create thin pages that simply repeat marketing copy with a different keyword.
